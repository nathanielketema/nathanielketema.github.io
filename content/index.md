# Welcome :)

![me](assets/me.png){.me width=25%} I am Nathaniel Ketema. I am an undergraduate student
in my final year at Dakota State University. As an undergraduate, I've been part of a team
that has partnered with the Department of Energy to work on problems ranging from path
optimization to post quantum cryptography. This work has led to a few
[published papers](#Published-papers).\

Outside of academia, I spend most of my free time at a coffee shop, sipping a cortado, and
working on a personal project for the joy of programming. I love building things from
scratch, understanding every layer, and minimizing complexity.

I currently live in South Dakota, so if you’re ever in the area, do reach out!

## Projects

- [shellu](https://github.com/nathanielketema/shellu): A POSIX compatible shell
  written in Zig.
- [znn](https://github.com/nathanielketema/znn): A neural network library.
- [personal site](https://github.com/nathanielketema/nathanielketema.github.io): A

- beam: A desktop application for a general contractor to manage project estimates,

- [breakaml](https://github.com/nathanielketema/breakaml): A Breakout game written in
  OCaml.
- [drug simulation](https://github.com/nathanielketema/monte-carlo-drug-simulation): A
